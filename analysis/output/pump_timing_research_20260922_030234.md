## Taxonomy

- **real pump** = peak_price/first_price - 1 >= 30%
- **winner** = real pump AND retained_fraction_of_peak_gain >= 20% (held at least a fifth of the peak gain)
- **failed pump** = real pump but gave back more than 80% of the peak gain
- **rug** = not a real pump AND final_lifetime_return <= -50%
- **flat/no-event** = everything else (no meaningful move either way)

- winner: n=180
- failed_pump: n=258
- rug: n=279
- flat_no_event: n=230
